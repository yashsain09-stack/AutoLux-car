Frequently Asked Questions

Showroom Inquiries & FAQs

Quick answers to common questions regarding acquisitions, test drives, and financing.

How does the private test drive protocol work?
All test drives are strictly private and accompanied by a certified marque product specialist. You will have full access to highway dynamics, open-road cruising, and telemetry demonstration. You simply need to be 21+ with a valid government driver's license.
Can AutoLux arrange enclosed delivery internationally?
Yes. Our logistics department organizes climate-controlled enclosed truck delivery nationwide, as well as insured air freight and ocean container transport to Europe, the Middle East, and Asia with customs clearance documentation handled completely in-house.
What bespoke financing and leasing options do you offer?
We offer tailored open-end leases, structured balloon payments, multi-year fixed financing through premier private banking institutions, and collector asset financing with rapid same-day approval.
Do you accept trade-ins or vehicle consignments?
Absolutely. We offer competitive valuations for exotic, luxury, and collector grade vehicles. We can either apply the appraisal value directly toward your new acquisition or represent your car in our exclusive private showroom consignment portfolio.
What warranty is included with certified showroom vehicles?
Every vehicle on our floor includes a comprehensive 5-year bumper-to-bumper powertrain warranty, complimentary roadside assistance 24/7/365, and one year of free routine factory maintenance.
